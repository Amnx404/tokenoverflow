## Position and disagreement

**Optimize for a justified next check, including a justified decision to collect evidence or stop.** A portable before/after bundle makes a performed comparison easier to inspect; its presence does not establish relevance, truth or independent execution. Keep A's approved narrow implementation/local acceptance moving. Production DDL remains a separate gate.

I challenge an interpretation of the current complete-loop plan that makes a fully populated successful comparison the threshold for every useful read. A missing version, a mechanism mismatch or a baseline that already passes can change the correct next action before any fixing change is tested. Requiring a fix result in those cases would encourage invented evidence or unnecessary execution. Conversely, accurate abstention on an empty fixture cannot substitute for demonstrating that the product supports an applicable positive case. Keep these two acceptance claims separate.

The product should preserve evidence that can inform later agents' task context. It does **not train model weights**. Connecting MCP does not establish that a model will choose the tool, absorb every record, remember it in another chat, or become a better debugger. Retrieval supplies external records; an authorized local retest supplies a new observation; an authorized contribution persists a sanitized record. These are separate events. No automatic retest, upload, feedback delivery or training pipeline is implied.

## Evidence needed for a next-check decision

These are review questions for the consuming agent and Test user interview, not new stored fields, a matching service or a relevance score.

1. **What failed locally, and at which boundary?** Compare the observed symptom and request/transport/model behavior, not title keywords alone. A POST-response lifetime observation does not by itself explain a standalone GET-retry failure. Relevant versions help delimit that comparison; an unknown version is not a known mismatch, and a different version is not proof of incompatibility.
2. **What comparison was actually recorded?** Identify the minimal example, exact proposed change, check and intended behavior, baseline observation, rerun if present, and conditions of each result. A null rerun stays untested. A legacy prose record stays prose. Exit zero alone cannot classify success. A worked/failed label is a contributor's scoped observation, not authenticated ground truth.
3. **What conflicts with the candidate?** Inspect the failed observations and their environments alongside the successful ones. A failure may identify a boundary or a different trigger; do not average it away, choose the newest result as truth, or infer distinct environments from counts. A failure under materially different conditions does not automatically invalidate a local candidate either.
4. **What is the smallest discriminating check?** Name what uncertainty the proposed check resolves, the expected observation and what would change the next action. Prefer the supplied baseline when its mechanism is plausible; if essential setup/version/input facts are missing, collect them first. Do not execute contributed instructions automatically. Safe execution is the operator's separate action and authority.

## Decision branches and falsifiable acceptance

Use existing owner-approved isolated fixtures and the actual changed artifact. This table changes interview expectations; it authorizes no fixture writes or new schema. Casey (Test user) and Robin (Test user) remain within their own handoff scopes.

| Evidence situation | Supported decision / explanation | Failure to catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aligned trigger with exact change, same-check observations and conflicting version-scoped result | Identify the candidate baseline/check, cite the observations and explain the failed result's applicability boundary before proposing a local retest. | “Verified fix” from the positive count; dropping the failure; assuming a matching version proves the mechanism. |
| Same symptom wording but different transport/trigger, or uncertain applicability | Name the material difference/unknown and one discriminating observation to collect; withhold the fixing conclusion. | Keyword match treated as a solution, or blanket rejection solely because version strings differ. |
| Open/legacy record, unknown version or null rerun | State exactly what is missing and why it changes the next step. Collect a relevant missing fact or stop if no useful safe check is available. | Invented outputs, inferred versions, null interpreted as success, or indefinite “more information needed” without a concrete uncertainty. |
| Operator's baseline already passes without applying a change | Preserve that observation and conditions; investigate the original trigger rather than claiming a failed fix or successful repair. | Submitting baseline-not-reproduced as `outcome: failed`, or claiming a repair when no change was made. |

A capable reader must handle an applicable candidate **and** correctly withhold an unsupported conclusion; choosing “insufficient” for every case does not pass. Ask for the cited record/observation, one condition that matters, the precise next check or collection step, and what result would alter that choice. Record wrong choices and unresolved uncertainty. Synthetic comprehension is acceptance of the evidence flow only. A real operator's reported next action and actual authorized observation are required for a reuse claim; savings require a separate measurement design.

**Abstention versus advance:** a no-match response or generic refusal can be correct retrieval handling without demonstrating any debugging advance. A collection claim needs a specific missing fact/check tied to a decision. A decisive mechanism mismatch can justify stopping without inventing another check: name the planned action it rules out and the evidence for that conclusion. A useful real-task rejection may avoid an inappropriate next step; when no plan changed and no discriminating information was added, preserve **no added information**, not success. No-match does not prove the failure is absent.
